YourDrink Privacy Policy

Applicable region: Outside Mainland China · Effective date: September 3, 2026

This policy applies to YourDrink Android app v0.12.1 distributed outside Mainland China. No account is required. Drink and alcohol logs are processed and stored only on the user’s device.

1. Purpose of the App

YourDrink is a personal drink logging and goal management app. It allows users to record drink types, amounts, notes, daily water goals, reminder preferences, and historical records.

The app may be used to record alcoholic drinks, but it does not sell, trade, recommend, advertise, or promote alcoholic products.

2. Personal Information

This version of YourDrink does not actively collect, upload, sell, rent, or share users’ personal information.

The app does not require account registration or login and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or cloud-sync services.

3. Data Stored on the Device

The following information may be stored locally on the user’s device:

  • Drink and alcohol logs, including drink name, category, amount, notes, record date, and internal creation timestamp.
  • Custom drinks, quick-add selections, categories, and default amounts.
  • Preferences such as daily water goal, reminder schedule, reminder interval, interface language, and achievement settings.
  • Necessary app state, including data format version, update notice state, and accepted privacy policy version.
  • Temporary widget summaries and locally generated widget images required to display Android home-screen widgets.

4. Storage, Use, and Sharing

App data is stored locally using Android application storage and WebView storage. It is used only to display records, calculate daily totals and goal progress, generate historical statistics, preserve user preferences, and update home-screen widgets.

YourDrink does not provide cloud synchronization and does not send drink records, goals, reminders, notes, or other locally stored data to the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

5. System Permissions and Special Access

YourDrink does not request access to location, contacts, existing photos, camera, microphone, phone calls, SMS messages, or precise device identifiers.

  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to restore user-enabled drink reminders and refresh home-screen widgets after the device restarts or the app is updated.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display drink reminder notifications after the user enables reminders and grants notification permission.
  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to improve the timing of fixed-time reminders after the user grants the relevant Android special access. If access is not granted, the app uses the inexact scheduling permitted by the system.

These permissions and special access are not used to read, collect, or upload drink records or other personal data.

The reminder diagnostics feature checks system states that Android allows the app to read and provides links to relevant notification, exact alarm, background activity, battery, and lock-screen settings. YourDrink does not silently change these settings.

6. Achievement Images

When the user selects “Save image,” YourDrink generates an achievement card as a PNG image on the device.

On Android 10 or later, the image is written through the Android system media library without reading the user’s existing photos. On earlier supported Android versions, the system document picker allows the user to choose where the image is saved.

Achievement images are created only after a user action. They are not uploaded, shared, or published automatically.

7. Backup and Restore

Users may export their records, custom drinks, and settings as an unencrypted ZIP backup file and import that file after reinstalling the app or changing devices.

Exported backup files are controlled and stored by the user. Because the ZIP file is not encrypted, users should keep it in a secure location and should not send it to untrusted persons or services.

8. Data Management and Deletion

  • Users can edit or delete individual drink records in the app.
  • Users can clear all local drink records from the Me page.
  • Users can manage or delete exported ZIP files and saved achievement images through the device’s file or gallery applications.
  • Uninstalling the app, clearing its application data, or changing devices may permanently remove locally stored records.

Because YourDrink does not maintain accounts, cloud storage, or server copies, the developer cannot remotely access, recover, modify, or delete data stored on a user’s device.

9. Children and Alcohol-Related Records

YourDrink is not designed specifically for children. Because the app supports personal logging of alcoholic drinks, app stores may apply age restrictions based on their regional policies.

The app does not provide alcohol purchasing, delivery, advertising, or promotional services.

10. Changes to This Policy

If app features, permissions, data processing practices, or applicable requirements materially change, this policy will be updated.

When a material policy change requires renewed consent, YourDrink will ask users to review and accept the updated policy before continuing to use the relevant app features.
